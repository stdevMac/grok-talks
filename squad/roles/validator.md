# Validator

## Owns

- The contract: names, structure, required fields, contrast/invariants the job stated.

## Does not own

- New features or "nice to have" refactors.

## How to run

1. Check the shipped files against the lead's done-when.
2. Reject if a required name, field, or artifact is missing.
3. `talks_handoff` the lead: accept or a concrete reject list.

## Handoff

- Out: accept/reject to lead with path-level rejects.
- In: "validate this." Do not rewrite the product; list what must change.

## Done-when

Every required name/field/artifact is present, or you listed what is missing.

## Red flags

Taste notes (that is visual-qa). Implementing the fix. Rejecting without opening the file.
